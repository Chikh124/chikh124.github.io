Building this portfolio

A dev diary of putting this site together, page by page.

Day one

One stylesheet, one look

Created the first HTML pages and connected one global stylesheet, so every page shares the same look.

Day two

A real navigation bar

Made the navigation bar one solid block at the top and pinned the footer to the bottom of the page.

Day three

Real content, not placeholders

Filled the pages with real content: my work at Zorgbalie, the VroegSignaleren project with HZ, and my freelance work.

Later

Containers, color, and an interactive dashboard

Rebuilt the navigation into a full-width sticky bar, split every page into separate containers, gave the blog four clickable topic pages instead of one long list, and turned the dashboard's empty grade column into real inputs with a progress bar that grows as I pass courses.

Side projects

VroegSignaleren, freelance, and weekend builds

The things I build outside of HZ assignments, and what I take away from each one.

VroegSignaleren

I'm the developer on VroegSignaleren, a product built together with researchers from HZ Vlissingen. It's the project where I get direct feedback from people who actually use what I ship, which is a different kind of pressure than a school assignment - in a good way.

Freelance: a business-optimisation tool

Next to VroegSignaleren I'm building a tool for a freelance client who wants to optimise their business processes. It started as a favor and turned into a real brief with a real deadline, which is exactly the kind of problem-first-then-sell approach I write about on the Why IT page.

Weekend builds

Smaller things I ship for fun: Telegram bots, little automations for my own planner job at Zorgbalie, and this portfolio itself. Most of them never go further than "it works on my machine," and that's fine - they're practice, not products.

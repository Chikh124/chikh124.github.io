My goals

What I want to get done this year, and a bit further out, based on the SWOT below. There's also a copy of this SWOT on the blog, next to the rest of my study notes.

My SWOT as a student

Strengths

  • Creative - I come up with a lot of ideas and new approaches.
  • Good problem solver - I find solutions quickly, even for difficult tasks.
  • I stay calm under pressure and don't panic when something goes wrong.

Weaknesses

  • I sometimes procrastinate and put tasks off until the last moment.
  • I can be lazy with tasks that don't feel important or urgent.
  • I don't always plan my week, so deadlines can pile up.

Opportunities

  • Build my network through events, meetups, teachers and fellow students.
  • Strengthen my programming basics with extra courses and side projects.
  • Find an internship at an interesting, innovative company.

Threats

  • Distractions like games, social media and my phone that eat up study time.
  • Strong competition from other students for good internships.
  • A heavy workload in busy periods, when several deadlines come at once.

This SWOT is what the two SMART goals below are built on: Goal 1 leans on the problem-solving strength above, and Goal 2 is a direct fix for the procrastination weakness. The internship opportunity and the competition threat are why "get the propedeuse" and "find an internship at an innovative company" are both on my list for this year.

My mission this year

This year, my mission is to turn my ideas and problem-solving skills into real results. My first milestone is delivering a finished product to my client in the second half of October, working on it 30 hours a week with clear weekly goals. At the same time, I am tackling my biggest weakness, procrastination, by planning every week and starting new tasks straight away. By becoming more disciplined and consistent, I want to hand in all my work on time and build a strong base for an internship at an innovative company.

SMART goals

Goal 1 - using a strength

I'm going to use my problem-solving skills and discipline to finish and deliver the product for my client in the second half of October 2026.

  • How: I work on the project 30 hours per week, split the remaining work into weekly milestones and check my progress with the client every Friday.
  • Measurable: all agreed features are built, tested and approved by the client before the deadline.

Goal 2 - fixing a weakness

I'm going to reduce my procrastination by making a weekly plan every Sunday evening, starting this week and continuing until the end of this semester.

  • How: I start every new assignment within 24 hours and work in 50-minute focus blocks with my phone in another room (Pomodoro method).
  • Measurable: I hand in 100% of my assignments on time and complete at least 80% of my planned weekly tasks, tracked in a to-do app.

This year

  • Get my propedeuse at HZ.
  • Find more freelance clients, as many as I can handle.
  • Keep shipping VroegSignaleren features with the HZ research team.
  • Finish the business-optimisation product I'm building for a client.

Later

  • Turn the freelance work into steady income.
  • Become properly confident with fullstack, not just backend.
  • Build by myself whole infrastracture for client's projects.
  • Set up a real CI/CD pipeline from scratch by myself.

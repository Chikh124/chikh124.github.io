Why I chose IT

Honestly, because I like making my own life easier. I automate and optimise the annoying parts of my work, and then I sell those solutions to other people who have the same problem.

I really believe that to sell a solution to a problem, you first have to run into that problem yourself. That's why I build tools for my own work first, and only then turn them into products.

What I enjoy the most

  • Automating boring, repetitive tasks.
  • Optimising processes so they take less time.
  • Vibe coding side projects at home.
  • Turning a small tool into something other people pay for.
